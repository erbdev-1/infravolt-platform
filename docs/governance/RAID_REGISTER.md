## Review rule

Each task and gate review must update affected entries with evidence, owner, status, and decision references. Closing an item requires recorded evidence; silence, implementation progress, or an unsupported assumption is not closure.

## Project-owner decision — GERSAN catalogue source material

On 21 August 2026, the Project Owner directed that GERSAN catalogue materials supplied inside this repository/project workspace are authorised source material for the InfraVolt website. This decision permits catalogue-derived product information, reference lists, customer/contractor/project names, locations, reference tables, company logos extracted directly from the catalogues, catalogue headings and structures, and technical catalogue assets already prepared for the website.

This repository governance decision supersedes the former RAID-002 publication block for that defined project material. It does not create or represent a separate external legal letter, signature, approval email, or GERSAN-issued evidence document, and it does not permit invented references, altered source relationships, downloaded replacement logos, or unsupported official relationship, territory, exclusivity, certification, or endorsement claims.

## Project-owner decision — public technical-resource downloads

On 21 August 2026, the Project Owner directed that the exact GERSAN catalogues supplied inside this repository/project workspace and faithful PDF derivatives of catalogue certificate, test-report, installation, drawing, table and system-overview pages may be offered as direct public downloads on the InfraVolt Technical Resources page.

This is a task-specific, source-limited public-download exception to the earlier controlled-document assumption. It permits stable public URLs for those repository-supplied catalogue materials and their content-preserving derivatives. It does not assert that a historical certificate remains current, does not authorise rewritten technical content or fabricated CAD/BIM/photometric files, and does not close the wider content-freshness and applicability risk in RAID-003.
